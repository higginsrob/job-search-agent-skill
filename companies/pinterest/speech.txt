Pinterest is a visual discovery and shopping platform where people save Pins, search for inspiration, and shop. It monetizes mainly through ads, with surfaces like Homefeed, Search, shopping Pins, and advertiser tools, and a 2026 A.I. push that includes conversational shopping, ads assistants, creative A.I., and agent tooling on top of its Taste Graph models.

On the fiscal side, Pinterest is public. Recent results show solid revenue growth, healthy free cash flow, and record monthly active users, with guidance that keeps growth in the mid-teens. Early twenty twenty-six restructuring cut under fifteen percent of staff and some offices to reallocate toward A.I. roles and go-to-market—not a hiring freeze.

Hiring is active despite that restructuring, concentrated on A.I. and M.L. platform, ads infrastructure, growth and discovery, data platforms, and developer-agent tooling. Open themes include staff and senior staff M.L. platform, growth A.I., serving and batch platforms, observability, and engineering managers for developer agents. Locations skew Bay Area, Seattle, remote U.S., with a newer Zurich hub.

For staff and similar roles, there is steady first-party A.T.S. flow with deep J.D.s, explicit years, stack detail, and published U.S. salary bands. The seniority bar is high. Remote often means U.S. remote with occasional collaboration near Bay Area or Seattle hubs. Ghost-job risk looks lower than secondary boards; the posture is selective A.I. and infra hiring rather than spray-and-pray volume.

Bottom line: Pinterest is a real, well-documented employer for staff-level A.I., platform, and tools work—expect a high bar and confirm Denver or remote eligibility on each posting.
