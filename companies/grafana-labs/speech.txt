Grafana Labs makes Grafana, the open dashboard for metrics, logs, and traces, and a commercial cloud and enterprise business on the same projects. Grafana Cloud is their managed OpenTelemetry platform, with Adaptive Telemetry for cost and a Knowledge Graph for root cause. The open source L G T M stack is Loki for logs, Grafana, Tempo for traces, and Mimir for Prometheus-compatible metrics. In twenty twenty-six they shipped agentic operations: Grafana Assistant, a Cloud M C P server, and Agent Observability. Grafana thirteen launched at Grafana Con in Barcelona in April, with a new Loki architecture and the Logline deal. C E O is co-founder Raj Dutt. C T O is Tom Wilkie.
